Export command for the issuing CA 
certutil -config "ca01.example.com\Example Issuing CA" -view -restrict "Disposition=20" `
  -out "RequestID,RequesterName,CommonName,NotBefore,NotAfter,CertificateTemplate,SerialNumber" csv |
  Out-File "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\adcs-issued.csv" -Encoding utf8

Disposition 20 is issued certificates. Any CSV with a subject and an expiry column also works, including Get-ChildItem Cert:\ exports and this page's own export. Site, owner, service and notes carry over to renewed certs with the same subject and template.
